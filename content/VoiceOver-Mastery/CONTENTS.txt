CONTENTS

This is the full first edition, written in November 2026.
Start with README.txt if you are new to the book, then Part 0.
iPhone is the longest part and teaches the shared VoiceOver ideas.
Later parts tell you what is the same on each other Apple platform
and what changes.

Every chapter is also included in VoiceOver-Mastery-complete.txt,
which is one searchable file.
