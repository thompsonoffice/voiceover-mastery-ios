VOICEOVER MASTERY: THE COMPLETE APPLE GUIDE
A book in SRTS: Screen Reader Training Suite
Part 3. Mac (macOS)
Chapter 3.7. Essential Keyboard Commands
============================================================

OVERVIEW
Mac VoiceOver has many command groups: general, interaction, orientation, navigation, web, text, and search. This book does not reprint every official table. It collects the essential commands already introduced, and it tells you how to find the rest safely.

Use Keyboard Help (VO-K) and the Commands menu (VO-H-H) as your complete reference on your Mac.


WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
- A compact list of essential official commands.
- How Quick Nav commands are turned on or off.
- How to find additional official commands without guessing.


ESSENTIAL COMMANDS
Turning VoiceOver on or off, and getting help:

- Command-F5, or Command plus three presses of Touch ID: turn VoiceOver on or off.
- Option-Command-F5, or three presses of Touch ID: Accessibility Shortcuts panel.
- VO-K: Keyboard Help.
- VO-H: Help menu.
- VO-H-H: Commands menu.
- VO-Fn-Command-F8: tutorial.
- VO-Question Mark: VoiceOver User Guide.
- VO-Fn-F8, or VO-Fn-8: VoiceOver Utility.

Moving and acting:

- VO-Left Arrow or VO-Right Arrow: previous or next item.
- VO-Space bar: activate.
- VO-Shift-Down Arrow: interact with a group.
- VO-Shift-Up Arrow: exit a group.
- VO-U: open the rotor.
- VO-V: verbosity rotor.

Reading and speech:

- Control: pause or resume speech.
- VO-Shift-N: hear the hint for the current item.
- VO-Shift-H: hear the help tag for the current item.
- VO-Fn-F7: date and time. Press F7 additional times for battery and network status, as listed in Chapter 3.2.

System and privacy:

- VO-N: Notifications menu.
- VO-Shift-Fn-F11: screen curtain.
- VO-Semicolon: lock or unlock the VO modifier.
- VO-Tab: ignore the next key combination.
- Escape or Fn-Tab: close a menu or rotor, or exit a mode.

Quick Nav, from the general commands list:

- VO-Q: turn single-key Quick Nav on or off.
- VO-Shift-Q: turn arrow-key Quick Nav on or off.

Other general commands Apple lists, if your keyboard supports them:

- VO-Clear: turn NumPad commander on or off, only if the keyboard has a designated Clear key.
- VO-Shift-K: turn the option to control VoiceOver with Option keys on or off.


HOW TO LEARN MORE COMMANDS
1. Press VO-H-H and browse the Commands menu.
2. Press VO-Shift-F to open the Find Commands menu, if you want to search.
3. Open the official VoiceOver User Guide with VO-Question Mark, then go to the command tables for interaction, orientation, navigation, web, text, or search.
4. Practice any new command in Keyboard Help first.

This approach is safer than memorizing a huge unofficial list. Commands can also be customized, which is another reason to check your Mac rather than assume a printed shortcut still matches.


PRACTICE EXERCISE
1. Recite, then perform, Command-F5, VO-arrows, VO-Space bar, VO-Shift-Down Arrow, and VO-Shift-Up Arrow.
2. Open the Commands menu with VO-H-H, find one command you do not know, note it, close the menu, and try it in Keyboard Help.
3. Toggle single-key Quick Nav with VO-Q, hear the result, then turn it back to the state you want.


QUICK REFERENCE SUMMARY
- The essentials are: Command-F5, VO-arrows, VO-Space bar, interact and exit, VO-U, VO-K, VO-H, VO-Fn-F8, Control to pause, and VO-Shift-Fn-F11 for the screen curtain.
- VO-Q and VO-Shift-Q toggle the two Quick Nav modes.
- Use the Commands menu and the official User Guide for the rest.
- Do not invent shortcuts. Confirm them in Keyboard Help.
