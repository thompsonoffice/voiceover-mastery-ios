Access Mastery Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

App: Access Mastery (iPhone and iPad). Developer: Hunter Thompson.

Summary

Access Mastery does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. The developer, Hunter Thompson, receives no data from the app.

Works offline

The guide is included in the app. Access Mastery makes no network requests of its own and works with no internet connection.

No accounts, ads, or tracking

There are no accounts, sign-ins, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party software development kits. There is no purchase link in the app.

What stays on your device

Your audience choice, reading progress, bookmarks, last-opened chapter, text size, and contrast setting are saved only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. They may be included in your own device backups, which Apple handles under its privacy policy.

Links

Some chapters cite Apple documentation. If you open one of those source links, it opens in your browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Access Mastery collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear in the app and on its web page with a new effective date.

App Store privacy label

Data Not Collected.
