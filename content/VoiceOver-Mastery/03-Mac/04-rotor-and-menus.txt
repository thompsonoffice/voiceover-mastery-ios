Part 3. Mac (macOS)
Chapter 3.4. The Rotor and VoiceOver Menus on Mac
============================================================

OVERVIEW
The VoiceOver rotor on Mac categorizes onscreen items, such as links on a webpage or app controls like buttons, and lets you jump directly to an item in that category. There is also a gestures rotor if you use a trackpad.

This chapter covers the keyboard rotor Apple documents in "Get started with VoiceOver," plus official menus that list items, commands, and notifications. The trackpad rotor details are in Chapter 3.6.


WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
- How to open the rotor with the keyboard and choose a category.
- How to jump to an item, or close the rotor without jumping.
- How the Commands, Notifications, and related menus help you find things.
- How the Verbosity rotor works, as Apple documents it.


OPEN THE ROTOR WITH THE KEYBOARD
Apple's getting-started page documents this method:

1. Press VO-U to open the rotor.
2. Use the Right Arrow and Left Arrow keys until you hear the category you want.
3. Use the Up Arrow and Down Arrow keys to move through the items in that category.
4. Press the Space bar when you hear the item you want. The VoiceOver cursor moves to it.
5. Or press Escape to exit the rotor without selecting an item.

The categories you hear depend on the window. On a webpage you might hear links or headings. In an app you might hear buttons or other controls.


THE VERBOSITY ROTOR
Apple's general commands list documents VO-V to open the Verbosity rotor. Then press Left Arrow or Right Arrow to choose a setting, and Up Arrow or Down Arrow to change the level for that setting. This is a quick way to change how much VoiceOver says without opening VoiceOver Utility.


MENUS THAT LIST COMMANDS AND ITEMS
Useful official menus include:

- VO-H: VoiceOver Help menu.
- VO-H-H: Commands menu.
- VO-Shift-F: Find Commands menu.
- VO-N: Notifications menu.

Apple's broader "VoiceOver commands and gestures" overview also mentions orientation and navigation tools such as listing elements in a window, moving to the desktop or menu bar, and window spots. Those extra commands live in the Commands menu and in the official command lists. If you need a command this book does not list, open the Commands menu or the Find Commands menu rather than guessing a shortcut.


CLOSE MENUS AND ROTORS
Press Escape, or Fn-Tab, to close a menu or rotor, stop an action, or exit a mode, according to the general commands list.


PRACTICE EXERCISE
1. Open a webpage in Safari.
2. Press VO-U, arrow to Headings or Links, arrow through the list, and press Space bar to jump to one item.
3. Press VO-U again, then Escape to close the rotor without jumping.
4. Press VO-H-H and move through the Commands menu to see what is available. Press Escape to close it.
5. Optional: press VO-V and explore one verbosity setting, then put it back if you do not want a change.


QUICK REFERENCE SUMMARY
- VO-U opens the rotor. Left or Right Arrow chooses a category. Up or Down Arrow moves through items. Space bar jumps. Escape cancels.
- VO-V opens the Verbosity rotor.
- VO-H is Help. VO-H-H is the Commands menu. VO-N is Notifications.
- Use the Commands menu to find official shortcuts this book does not attempt to memorize all of.
- Escape or Fn-Tab closes a menu or rotor.
