Access Mastery

An offline guide to VoiceOver, the screen reader built into Apple devices, for iPhone and iPad. From turning VoiceOver on for the first time to working like a power user, on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro.

Written for blind and low-vision people, and for sighted people learning VoiceOver to teach or support someone.

What's inside

  • 54 chapters, about 48,000 words, built into the app
  • iPhone (25 chapters), iPad (5), Mac (9), Apple Watch (4), Apple TV (3), Apple Vision Pro (3), plus an opening part and a closing part on apps, practice, and sources
  • Every chapter has an overview, numbered steps with one action each, a practice exercise, a quick reference summary, and sources

Features

  • Browse by device, full-text search, quick reference, and a practice screen with real controls
  • Bookmarks, reading progress, and Resume
  • Large text sizes and a stronger contrast option

Private and offline

No account, no ads, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. The app collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
