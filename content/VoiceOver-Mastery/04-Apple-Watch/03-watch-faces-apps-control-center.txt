Part 4. Apple Watch (watchOS)
Chapter 4.3. Watch Faces, Apps, Control Center, and Notifications
============================================================

OVERVIEW
Once VoiceOver is on, you still use the Digital Crown and side button for many system jobs, sometimes with a VoiceOver twist. This chapter follows Apple's "Apple Watch basics with VoiceOver" page, plus general Digital Crown and side button behavior where Apple documents it.

Some of these steps assume you are viewing the current watch face.


WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
- How to change and customize a watch face with VoiceOver.
- How to open notifications, Control Center, widgets, and the App Switcher.
- How to open apps, including with Siri.
- How Apple describes reading Mail with the Digital Crown.


CHANGE AND CUSTOMIZE A WATCH FACE
While viewing the current watch face:

- To change the watch face: triple-tap the screen, turn the Digital Crown to browse available faces, then press the Digital Crown to choose the face.
- To customize a watch face: triple-tap the display, swipe down to choose Edit, then double-tap. Swipe left or right with two fingers to browse the customizable features. Turn the Digital Crown to customize the selected feature. When you finish, press the Digital Crown, then double-tap the face to save your changes.


NOTIFICATIONS, CONTROL CENTER, AND WIDGETS
From the watch face:

- Check notifications: swipe down with two fingers.
- From most other screens, Apple says to tap the time in the top-right of the display, then swipe down with two fingers.
- Open Control Center: press the side button. This matches Apple's general Apple Watch control for Control Center.
- Show widgets: from the watch face, scroll down with the Digital Crown or swipe up with two fingers.


OPEN THE APP SWITCHER AND APPS
- Open the App Switcher: double-click the Digital Crown, then turn the Digital Crown to scroll through recent apps. Double-tap an app to open it.
- Open any app in Grid View: press the Digital Crown once to go to the Home Screen. Turn the Digital Crown to scroll through apps. Drag your finger to highlight an app, then double-tap to open it.
- Or use Siri: hold down the Digital Crown until you feel the double tap, then say "launch" followed by the app name, such as "launch Mail."


READ MAIL, AS APPLE DESCRIBES IT
Apple's basics page gives this example:

1. Press the Digital Crown once to go to the Home Screen.
2. Swipe to highlight the Mail app, then double-tap, or hold the Digital Crown and say "launch Mail."
3. When Mail opens, turn the Digital Crown to navigate and hear messages.
4. Navigate through the message with the Digital Crown.

You can use the same open-and-scroll idea in other apps, then switch to swipes and the rotor when you need characters, words, or actions.


SIDE BUTTON AND OTHER HARDWARE, BRIEFLY
Apple's general "Use buttons and gestures" page, which is not VoiceOver-specific, documents the side button for Control Center (press), Emergency SOS (press and hold), Apple Pay (double-click), and power (press and hold). Those hardware actions still matter when VoiceOver is on. If a press-and-hold starts SOS accidentally, cancel using the onscreen controls VoiceOver speaks.

This book does not invent VoiceOver-only versions of Apple Pay or SOS. Use the official Apple Watch User Guide for those features.


PRACTICE EXERCISE
1. From the watch face, open notifications with a two-finger swipe down, then go back.
2. Press the side button for Control Center, explore a control without changing something important, then close it using the method your watch offers (often the side button again, or a swipe or scrub; follow what you hear rather than a guessed close gesture if Apple does not state one).
3. Double-click the Digital Crown for the App Switcher, then open an app with a double tap.
4. Press the Digital Crown for the Home Screen and open Settings or Clock by exploring and double-tapping.
5. Optional: browse watch faces with a triple-tap, but press the Digital Crown only when you hear the face you want to keep.


QUICK REFERENCE SUMMARY
- Triple-tap the watch face to change or edit faces. Turn the Digital Crown to browse. Press the crown to choose. Customize with Edit, two-finger swipes, and the crown, then press the crown and double-tap to save.
- Two-finger swipe down: notifications from the watch face.
- Side button: Control Center.
- Digital Crown down or two-finger swipe up: widgets from the watch face.
- Double-click the Digital Crown: App Switcher. Single press: Home Screen or watch face, depending on context.
- Hold the Digital Crown for Siri, then say "launch" and the app name.
